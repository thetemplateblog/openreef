How it measures

Three textbook methods, one small box.

Alkalinity by Gran titration

A 20 mL sample is titrated with 0.02 N HCl in 60 µL steps while a lab-grade electrode tracks pH. The excess-acid region is extrapolated to the equivalence point, with a DIC correction, so electrode offset drops out of the result.

Method & math →

Phosphate by molybdenum blue

The cell is read before and after reagent so turbidity and colour cancel. An 850 nm LED and an 11-channel spectral sensor resolve 0.01 ppm PO₄ in a 22 mm cuvette.

Optics & chemistry →

Nitrate by azo dye

Nitrate is reduced and coupled into a pink azo dye read at 540 nm. Reagent kinetics are characterised on your own standards, so any liquid kit — Trident NP or lab-mixed — can be profiled.

Hardware

Five printed syringe pumps, two cells, one Pi.

Every part is something you can order today. The syringe pumps are a NEMA17, a T8 lead screw and a 5 mL glass syringe between two check valves — 0.07 µL per step. Six cheap peristaltic pumps move sample and waste; a dip tube sets the volume so no pump needs to be precise except the ones that are.

  • Raspberry Pi 4, Atlas Scientific EZO-pH, AMS AS7341
  • Laser-cut acrylic enclosure, 420 × 460 × 200 mm, wet and dry bays
  • Leak sensor, waste float, reagent accounting, dry-contact alarm
  • OpenSCAD sources, panel DXF/SVG, wiring diagram, costed BOM

Software

A real API, not a web page with buttons.

One Python service runs the fluidics, the chemistry math, a scheduler, SQLite history and the dashboard. Every result keeps its raw titration curve or absorbance channels and a quality score. A hardware simulator with a carbonate-system model runs the whole stack on a laptop.

  • REST + WebSocket, OpenAPI schema, API-key auth
  • Hydros developer API rules and a hardware alarm contact
  • MQTT with Home Assistant discovery, webhooks, Prometheus
  • Calibration wizard: pH buffers, acid standardisation, curve fits, reagent characterisation

Build path

A weekend of printing, a weekend of plumbing.

  1. 01

    Order & print

    Order the BOM, print five pump frames, two cell parts and the brackets, cut the panels.

  2. 02

    Wire & flash

    Pi, drivers, I²C sensors and MOSFET board on the carrier; run deploy/install.sh.

  3. 03

    Plumb & prime

    Cells, dip tubes, check valves, reagent lines. Prime each pump from the dashboard.

  4. 04

    Calibrate & schedule

    pH buffers, Na₂CO₃ standard, two colorimetric standards each. Then let it run.
